## **Switch-On Diet Rules**

### **Week 1**

* 
**Allowed Foods:** [Soy sauce, turmeric, wasabi, red chili powder, perilla oil, green tea, avocado, cabbage, eggs, chicken, carrots, tofu, garlic, radish, multi-grain rice, coconut oil, mushrooms, broccoli, fish, sashimi, boiled pork (suyuk), vinegar, brown rice, black pepper, onions, soft tofu, olive oil, cucumber, plain yogurt, seafood, seaweed, herb tea] 


* **Rules:**
* **Days 1–3:** This is a gut-resting period. Provide nutrients solely through **protein shakes** to improve the body's metabolism. 


* **Days 4–7:** Have a **low-carb meal for lunch**. Consume half a bowl of multi-grain rice with a protein-rich side dish and minimize carbohydrates. 


* Drink more than **2 liters of water** daily. 


* Complete dinner **4 hours before bedtime**. 





### **Week 2**

* 
**Allowed Foods:** [All Week 1 foods + legumes, nuts, black coffee, milk, cheese] 


* **Rules:**
* Perform a **24-hour fast once a week**. 


* Consume **protein shakes twice a day** and eat **two regular meals**. 


* 
**Do not consume carbohydrates** for dinner. 


* Drink more than **2 liters of water** daily. 


* Complete dinner **4 hours before bedtime**. 





### **Week 3**

* 
**Allowed Foods:** [All Week 2 foods + sweet pumpkin, tomatoes, bananas, berries, sweet potatoes] 


* **Rules:**
* Perform a **24-hour fast twice a week**, but do not do them on consecutive days. 


* Consume **protein shakes twice a day** and eat **two regular meals**. 


* 
**Do not consume carbohydrates** for dinner. 


* Incorporate **high-intensity exercise** to increase muscle mass. 


* Drink more than **2 liters of water** daily and complete dinner **4 hours before bedtime**. 





### **Week 4**

* 
**Allowed Foods:** [All Week 3 foods + fruits] 


* **Rules:**
* Perform a **24-hour fast three times a week**, but do not do them on consecutive days. 


* 
**Eat fruit** in the morning. 


* Have **low-carb meals** for both lunch and dinner. 


* Drink more than **2 liters of water** daily and complete dinner **4 hours before bedtime**.
